Separate aliases with semicolons. Matching uses normalized whole names/words, so Ration will match ration items without matching unrelated names such as Integration.

Examples: Rations, 1 week; Rations; Ration

The whole-word alias Water also matches names such as 20 Gallon Half-Barrel of Water.

Charged items are preferred over quantity-only items. For water, one charge of capacity is treated as one gallon.
