WWN travel defaults

Global ration/water item aliases now live in Configure Settings → Module Settings → Road Provisions. Matching uses whole names/words, charged items are preferred, and partially-used bundles/containers are consumed before full ones. For water, configure containers with 1 charge of capacity per gallon (for example 1 for a waterskin or 20 for a half-barrel); the module treats WWN charges as capacity vs. expended and leaves emptied containers in inventory.
